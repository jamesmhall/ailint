# AIL100: no-vague-instruction

**Category**: [Semantic](README.md)  
**Default Severity**: `Warning`

## Overview
Detects instructions using generalized, passive, or non-deterministic phrasings like "do your best," "be helpful," "properly," or "best practices."

## Why it matters
AI agents act as literal compilers around prompts. They have no deterministic understanding of what you mean by "best practices" in your specific context unless you define what those practices are. Non-committal language wastes context tokens.

## How to fix it
Replace passive/general instructions with absolute imperatives. 

**Bad:**
> Try to log errors properly.

**Good:**
> Log all caught exceptions using `tracing::error!` rather than `println!`.
